2. What we collect and why

Quote and contact requests

When you send our quote form, we receive your name, email address, the service you are interested in, your budget range (optional) and your message. We use this only to reply to you and prepare an offer.

Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).

The form is delivered to our inbox through the form service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which processes the message on our behalf and may keep a copy for up to 30 days.

Online payments

When you buy a service, payment is handled by Stripe. You enter your card details on Stripe’s secure checkout page; we never see or store your full card number. We receive confirmation of the payment, the amount and the contact details you give Stripe.

Legal basis: performing our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and complying with accounting and tax law (Art. 6(1)(c)). Stripe’s own privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

Website hosting

Our hosting provider, [hosting company], may record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to keep the website secure and running. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Fonts

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. To deliver them, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address.
